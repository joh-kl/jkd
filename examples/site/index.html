04lab: type that reacts

Five processes, no resizing.

Each experiment borrows a real process: a tracker, a dot screen, a scanner, a press, a composing stick. Scroll through each one; move the pointer. When nothing moves, the type settles and reads.

04.1 blob tracking

Every shape the pixels form is found, boxed and numbered, the way a TouchDesigner tracker sees a dancer.

04.2 moiré, after Weingart

The word exists only as a shift between two dot screens. Aligned, it reads. Turned, it dissolves into fringes.

04.3 slit-scan

Cut into bands, each a moment behind the one above. Still, it is crisp. Scroll fast and it smears like a scanner.

04.4 misregistration

Two plates, one word. Speed pulls the halftone plate out of register; stopping snaps it back.

04.5 the composing stick

Set by hand, one sort at a time, mirrored, with the spacing quads in grey. Then proofed, and it reads.
