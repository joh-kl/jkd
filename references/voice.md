# Voice

How JKD things speak, in interfaces, slides and print. Personality as pairs,
each with its limit:

- **Direct, but not curt.** Say the thing, then stop.
- **Plain, but not dull.** Everyday words; one vivid word beats three
  adjectives.
- **Confident, but not boastful.** State what it does; let numbers carry claims.
- **Playful, but not in the way.** Jokes live in Easter eggs and celebrations,
  never in an error or a confirmation.

## Rules

- **No dashes as punctuation**, anywhere a person reads: use a colon, a comma,
  a full stop or a middle dot. (A dash inside a name or in data stays.)
- **Concise.** If a word can go, it goes. Captions are fragments.
- **Numbers over sentences.** "3 of 4" beats "You own three of the four".
- **One name per action.** The button says "File", the result says "Filed",
  History says "filed". Do not switch to "move" or "save" halfway.
- **Errors** say what happened and what to do next: "That file is no longer
  where it was." Never blame, never "Oops".
- **Confirmations** name exactly what will happen, with the count: "Remove 12
  cards?" with one destructive button and Cancel. The scope was chosen before
  the dialog, not in it.
- **Plain words for machinery**: "memory" not "embeddings", "fingerprint" not
  "vector", "guess" not "inference".
- **Plurals** are computed, never "file(s)".
- **Languages.** Interface in English unless the product is German; German texts
  use « » and Swiss spelling (ss, not ß).
- **Capitalisation**: lowercase captions and page titles (brand), sentence case
  for text, title case for Mac buttons and menus, sentence case for iOS buttons.
