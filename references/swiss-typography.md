# Swiss typography in JKD

The International Typographic Style (Zurich and Basel, 1950s on: Müller-Brockmann,
Ruder, Hofmann) is the centre of JKD. These are the rules as they apply here.

## The grid

- Start every layout from the grid, not from the content. Columns, gutters and
  margins first (values in `DESIGN.md › layout`), then place.
- **A baseline rhythm**: text sits on multiples of 4 pt. Leading is chosen so a
  line lands on the rhythm (13 pt text on 18, 17 on 24, 20 on 28).
- Elements align to column edges and to each other's baselines and tops. If two
  things nearly align, make them align.
- **Asymmetric balance.** Heavy elements (a headline, an image) on one side,
  white space on the other. Centred, symmetrical layouts are the exception.
- White space is not empty. Leave whole columns free; do not fill to the edge
  because space is available.

## The type

- **One grotesk.** Switzer, a neo-grotesk in the line of the büro guide's
  Akzidenz Grotesk, from Thin to Black. No second family for "personality": personality
  comes from size, weight and placement.
- **Few sizes, big jumps.** Use the scale (11, 13, 17, 20, 28, 36, 72, 108). A
  headline should be clearly bigger, not a little bigger. Two or three sizes on
  one screen is plenty.
- **Weight carries hierarchy** with size: bold headings, regular text, medium
  labels. Never colour alone.
- **Flush left, ragged right.** No justification (it opens rivers), no centred
  body text. Watch the rag: avoid a single short word on the last line of a
  heading; break headlines by meaning ("Necessity / Fueling / Creative / Drive").
- **Stacked headlines** are a büro and Swiss move: one or two words per line,
  bold, tight leading (1.0), aligned to one edge. Right aligned stacks are fine
  on a poster when they sit against the right margin.
- **Lowercase** for captions, page labels and page titles in app chrome (the
  guide's "final logotype", "brand applications"). Sentence case for text.
  Platform capitalisation for buttons and menus.
- **Line length** 45 to 75 characters for reading text.
- **Tracking**: negative on display sizes (-0.8 at 36, -2 at 72), slightly
  positive on small lowercase captions (+0.4). Never letterspace body text.
- **Figures**: tabular for anything that changes or lines up in a column.
- **Quotes and marks**: typographic quotes (« » in German texts, “ ” in English),
  a real × for dimensions, the ellipsis character …

## Images

- Grayscale architecture and clean studio photographs, as in the guide.
  Objective, not decorative: the subject fills the frame, the horizon is level.
- Images align to the grid: full bleed to an edge, or exactly on columns.
- Text over an image only where the image is quiet and contrast is measured.
  A halftone treatment is the JKD way to put type on a photo (see
  `expressive.md`).

## Do and don't

- Do let one element be very large. Scale contrast is the Swiss signature.
- Do use rules (hairlines) to structure, sparingly.
- Don't box everything. A slab is for what you act on.
- Don't mix alignments in one block.
- Don't use more than one bold line in a row of small text.
- Don't use drop shadows with blur, gradients behind text, or rounded
  "friendly" shapes.
